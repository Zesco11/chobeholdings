The Legacy

Founded on a vision of
sustainable discovery.

Established in 1982 and headquartered in Gaborone, Chobe Holdings Limited has grown into Botswana's premier ecotourism group. We are proud to be the only tourism entity listed on the Botswana Stock Exchange, operating iconic brands including Desert & Delta Safaris and Ker & Downey Botswana.

Our journey began with the historic Chobe Game Lodge—the only permanent luxury lodge situated inside Chobe National Park. Today, we manage a portfolio of fourteen world-class camps and lodges across Botswana and Namibia.
