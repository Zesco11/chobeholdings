Our Mission

Chobe Holdings Limited is Botswana’s premier ecotourism company. Our mission is to provide outstanding wildlife and hospitality experiences by investing in people, wildlife, and Botswana. Since 1984, we have pioneered sustainable tourism, ensuring that our environmental policy minimizes our ecological footprint while enhancing the natural beauty of the regions in which we operate. By prioritizing conservation efforts and empowering local communities, we ensure that every safari is a privilege that preserves the natural world for future generations.

Silent Safaris

In 2014, we launched the first electric-powered safaris in Botswana. Using advanced technology for solar-powered boats and vehicles, we offer a smooth, silent connection with nature that significantly reduces carbon emissions and noise pollution in sensitive habitats.

Community Botho

At the heart of our operations is the philosophy of 'Botho'—promoting the common good of society. We lead the way through youth development programs, female empowerment initiatives, and staff profit-share schemes, ensuring Botswana's citizens remain the core owners of our success.
